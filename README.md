# Horizon

A personalized, AI-powered Social Security claiming-age planner — see what
your benefit is worth at every age from 62 to 70, based on your real numbers.

## Structure
- `docs/` — product requirements
- `landing/` — marketing site
- `app/` — the React application (calculator, and eventually accounts, AI assistant, etc.)

Informational estimates only. Not financial, legal, or tax advice. Not
affiliated with or endorsed by the Social Security Administration.
